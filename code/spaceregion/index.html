Drag on the video to draw a region. Its X and Y are two continuous controls: r1x r1y for the first region, r2x r2y for the second, and so on — use them anywhere in the sequence above, then press play (Ctrl/Cmd + Enter re-runs it). The same movement is sent as MIDI CC to the output chosen at the top right of the video. With hands on, the music only plays while an open hand is in frame — make a fist and it fades out.
